## 👋 About me

I'm a software engineer who builds full-stack products and uses AI as a core part of how they're built and what they do. I'm a final-year student at **PDPM IIITDM**, and I'm currently a **Software Engineering (AI) intern at MailEasy / BettermindLabs**.

## 🚀 Featured projects

### [Blueprint – AI Product Manager](https://github.com/Mayank1Deshmukh/AI-Product-Manager) &nbsp;·&nbsp; [🌐 Live demo](https://ai-product-manager-chi.vercel.app)
Turns a startup idea into a BRD, PRD, and market analysis in under 20 seconds using parallel Gemini calls, with live competitor research, refinement, and export. Auth and per-user data are handled with Supabase Row Level Security.

<img src="https://skillicons.dev/icons?i=nextjs,ts,supabase,tailwind,vercel" alt="Blueprint tech stack" />

### [QuantiForge](https://github.com/Mayank1Deshmukh/QuantiForge)
A low-code platform where students design, train, and compare AI paper-trading strategies without writing code. Live training progress streams to the browser over WebSockets, with optional cloud training on a Dockerized GPU worker.

<img src="https://skillicons.dev/icons?i=nextjs,ts,fastapi,py,pytorch,sqlite,docker" alt="QuantiForge tech stack" />

### [Midnight Doomscroll Intensity](https://github.com/Mayank1Deshmukh/midnight-doomscroll-intensity)
An end-to-end ETL pipeline that turns raw app-usage and clickstream events into daily scrolling patterns, containerized with Docker and PostgreSQL and visualized in Tableau.

<img src="https://skillicons.dev/icons?i=py,postgres,docker" alt="ETL pipeline tech stack" />
